---
title: Play next, Add to queue and Repeat
group: Run a session
words: queue add to queue play next up next list order repeat repeat one loop room skip clear saved
---
Each room plays a list for the evening: the scene's music (or a playlist) in its order, and what you add to it.

- **Play next**, on any track or playlist in Live, plays it right after the track playing now.
- **Add to queue** puts it at the bottom of the room's list, after everything already there.
- **Up next**, in the room's part of On air, shows the list from the next track to the bottom. **×** takes one out for this evening; **Clear added** takes out everything you added.
- Starting another scene clears what you added. That scene's **Undo** brings it back.

There are two repeats.

- **Repeat** in On air, and on the Now playing bar, is for what plays in that room now: off, all, then one. It is never saved and ends when that room's music stops.
- **Repeat one** on a track of a scene is saved with the scene: the track plays again when it ends, every time the scene plays. A pressed one has a small dot.

With **Repeat all**, what you added comes round again with the rest. A scene's music always starts again from its top.
