**Filters**: click a filter once to show only those tracks (✓), twice to hide them (struck out), a third time to clear it. "Tense · not Combat" at the top of the box says what is on. **Clear all** clears every filter. Folders work the same way: once shows only that folder, twice hides it.

The **order** button at the top of Filters sorts the filters: tag list order, A to Z, Z to A, or most tracks first.

**Columns** chooses what the list shows: artist, album, type, use, tags, shape, energy, length, **Plays for** (the length after the Audio profile's start and end), added, folder. Click a column's name to sort by it; click again for the other way.
