Live is where you run the evening. **Run this session** on a campaign or session page loads it here.

- **Scenes**: press `Space`, or **Next scene**, to start the next scene. Click a scene's name to see what it plays before you start it. Changed your mind? **Undo**, or `Ctrl+Z`, within 8 seconds.
- **Current scene**: the scene that runs, its music in the order it plays, its ambience and its pads. Its pads answer to their keys.
- **On air**: a part for each room, with what it plays, **Up next**, its volume and its bot.
- **This session** and **Whole campaign**: their playlists and pads, to play a whole playlist, one of its tracks or a pad at any time.
- **Find a track** (`/`): search the whole library for anything not in the plan.
- **Fade all** (`Shift+Esc`) fades everything out; **Stop all** stops at once.

A room is a voice channel with one of your bots in it. Scenes always start in the first room. With two rooms or more, **To:** on a box chooses where it plays. **No room** or **Nobody there** means the players hear nothing: the button beside it puts the bot where the players are.

`Ctrl+Z` takes back only the last thing you did, while its **Undo** shows.

**Keys** lists every key. If single keys get in your way, turn **Single-key shortcuts** off there; `Shift+Esc` and `Ctrl+Z` still work.
