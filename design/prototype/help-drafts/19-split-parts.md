---
title: Split a track into parts
group: Prepare
words: split parts marker markers cut section audio profile
---
On a track's **Audio profile**, add a marker where each part should start, then press **Split into parts…** in the Markers box.

Each part becomes a track of its own that plays its part of the same file, with its own name, tags and audio profile. Your file is not changed, and the whole track stays in your library. Untick a split point to keep two parts together.

A part's details show which track it is part of, and the whole track's details list its parts.
