The **Now playing** bar has **Back**, **Forward** and **Queue**, for what the players hear and for a preview alike.

- **Back** goes to the previous track; after the first 3 seconds it goes to the start of this one.
- **Queue** opens the list: what played, what plays now and what comes next. **Play** on any row plays it now; the list carries on from there. `Esc` closes it.
- Previewing a playlist plays it through in your headphones, in its play order.

In Live, each room's **Up next** has **Play now** on its rows.
