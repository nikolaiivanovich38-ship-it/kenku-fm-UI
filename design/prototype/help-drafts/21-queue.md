The **Now playing** bar has **Back**, **Forward** and **Queue**, for what the players hear and for a preview alike.

- Press **Back** to go to the previous track. After the first 3 seconds of a track, pressing **Back** starts that track again instead.
- Press **Queue** to open the list of what played, what plays now and what comes next. Click **Play** on a row to play it now, and the list continues from there. To close the list, press `Esc`.
- Previewing a playlist plays it through in your headphones, in its play order.

In Live, each room's **Up next** has **Play now** on its rows.
