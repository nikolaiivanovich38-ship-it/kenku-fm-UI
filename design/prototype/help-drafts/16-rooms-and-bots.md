A room is a Discord voice channel Lyre plays into. A bot is the Discord account that plays the sound there. Each bot can be in one room at a time.

During play, you do everything with rooms in Live:
- To add a room, press **+ Room** in On air and pick a voice channel. Lyre uses a bot that is not in a room yet.
- Press a room's name to move the room to another channel or make its bot leave. From the same menu you can copy what another room plays, or change the room's bot to one that is not in a room yet.
- To take the bot out of the channel, press **Leave**. If you press **Undo** (or `Ctrl+Z`) within 8 seconds, the bot comes back with what it was playing.
- Each room has its own volume and **Mute**.

Before play, you get the bots ready in **Settings › Discord bots**:
- **Add a bot** has the steps for making one in Discord and pasting its token.
- **Starting volume** is the volume a bot starts at when you add it to a room.
- To use a new token, press **Replace…**. Rename or remove a bot from its **⋯** menu.
- **Rooms** shows which bot is in which voice channel, on one screen. It is also in the menu, under **Settings**.

Bots join a room only when you add them to it. The one time a bot joins by itself is after a dropped connection, when it goes back to the room it was in.
