title: A track's details anywhere
group: Prepare

Click a track's name in a playlist, a scene, Add music or Live's **All details** and its details open in the **Track panel** on the right, beside what you are doing. They are the same details as in the Library: change the title, tags, **Use it for** and **At its end** there.

- **Open in Library** shows it in the whole list, with its filters.
- **×** or `Esc` closes the panel.

On the Audio profile, the trail shows where you came from (*Playlists › Crypt ambush ›*), and the track's name goes back to its details there.
