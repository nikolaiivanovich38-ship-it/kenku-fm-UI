title: A track's details anywhere
group: Prepare
words: track details panel title tags playlist scene open library breadcrumb trail audio profile
---
Click a track's name in a playlist, a scene, Add music or Live's **All details** and its details open in the **Track panel** on the right, beside what you are doing. They are the same details as in the Track library, so you can change the title, tags, **Use it for** and **At its end** there.

- With **Open in Track library** you see the track in the whole list, with its filters.
- Press **×** or `Esc` to close the panel.

On the Audio profile, the trail shows where you came from (*Playlists › Crypt ambush ›*), and you can click the track's name to go back to its details.
