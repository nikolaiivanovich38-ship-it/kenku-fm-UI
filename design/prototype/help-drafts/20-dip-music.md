When an effect plays, Lyre can make the music quieter for a moment, so a thunder crack or a roar is heard. While the music is dipped, the room's music line in On air shows **Dipped**.

- **Settings › Sound to Discord › Dip the music under effects**: how far (Off, A little, A lot), how fast it comes back, and whether ambience dips too.
- On each pad, choose **While it plays**: *Dip the music* or *Don't dip*. Pads that play once dip the music. Loops and hold-to-play pads don't, unless you choose *Dip the music*.
