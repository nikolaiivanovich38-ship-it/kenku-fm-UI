# Round 10: what to try (the final design for 0.6.0)

Open `lyre-prototype.html` in a browser. It has no sound. The strip at the very top is the prototype's own (theme,
window size, design notes, and **Drop the connection** to show a lost bot); it is not part of Lyre. Start with
**Screen: Whole window**, then try **Half of a 1920 screen** for Live.

The number in brackets is the item on your list of 14 Nov, or your answer to the reviewers' questions of 18 Nov (Q1 to
Q7).

## Live

| # | Try this | You should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Live › press `Space`, then fire **Thunder crack** (or press a pad's key). | The boxes under On air do not move. The room's Effects line fills in; the music line says **Dipped** (47). |
| 2 | Watch the top strip after `Space`. | **Undo** shows in one place in the strip, never inside a box. `Ctrl+Z` does the same. A pad press leaves it there. |
| 3 | **Stop all**, then **Undo**. | Every room comes back where it was (Q6). If a preview was playing in your headphones, Stop all stopped it too; the button still says Stop all (Q7). |
| 4 | **Find a track** (`/`), "Brutal battle", **Play on air**; then **Undo**. | The scene's track comes back at its place. |
| 5 | Look at the music line in On air. | The title on up to two lines, then where it comes from and how it loops. Buttons: Previous · **Pause** (round, filled) · Next, a gap, Stop, then Shuffle, Repeat, Fade. The ambience row lines up under it. |
| 6 | Top strip: **Drop the connection**. | The room says **Connection lost · players hear nothing**, in amber, with **Try again** (and the other bot if one is free). Try again brings it back (56). |
| 7 | **Arrange**. Drag a box by its head; drag its foot; open its **Move…** menu. | Each box, On air too, has a grip, a foot to drag and one Move… menu. On air's Move… has **Into column 1/2/3**; then **To the top, the whole width**. **Done** keeps it for this window size; **Put back the usual layout** (44). |
| 8 | On a row in Current scene, point at the buttons. | Preview (headphones, only you) first; **Play on air** last, apart, red on hover: "Players hear this now". |
| 9 | Help (F1) › **Tour of this screen**. | Six steps: Next scene, On air, Preview, Pads, Undo, Fade all and Stop all (19). |
