# Round 10: what to try (the final design for 0.6.0)

Open `lyre-prototype.html` in a browser. It has no sound. The strip at the very top is the prototype's own (theme,
window size, design notes, and **Drop the connection** to show a lost bot); it is not part of Lyre. Start with
**Screen: Whole window**, then try **Half of a 1920 screen** for Live.

The number in brackets is the item on your list of 14 Nov, or your answer to the reviewers' questions of 18 Nov (Q1 to
Q7). Rows 26 to 29 are your four answers to this round's questions (A1 to A4).
