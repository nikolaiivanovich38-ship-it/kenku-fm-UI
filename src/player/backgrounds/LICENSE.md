# Background pictures

The 23 pictures in this folder (`autumn.jpg` … `winter.jpg`) are original to
Cue Desk. They were painted by code in this repository,
`scripts/backgrounds/paint.mjs`, and contain no photographs, downloaded images,
third-party artwork, fonts or text. They are distributed under the project's
licence, the GNU General Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0); the full text is
in `LICENSE` at the top of the repository.

Their file names are the background names Kenku FM stored on playlists and
soundboards (including Kenku FM's spelling "butterflys"), so libraries brought
over from Kenku FM show a picture without any change to their data. They are
not Kenku FM's pictures and do not copy them.

To rebuild them: `node scripts/backgrounds/render.mjs` (see the comments at the
top of that file).
