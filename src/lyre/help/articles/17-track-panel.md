title: A track's details anywhere
group: Prepare

Click a track's name in a playlist, a scene or Live and its details open in a panel on the right, beside what you are doing. They are the same details as in the Track library, so you can change the title, tags, **Use it for** and **At its end** there.

- With **Open in Track library** you see the track in the whole list, with its filters.
- Press **×** or `Esc` to close the panel.

On the Audio profile, the top bar shows where you came from. From a playlist, for example, it shows **Playlists**, the playlist's name and **Track details**. Press **Track details** to go back to the track's details.
