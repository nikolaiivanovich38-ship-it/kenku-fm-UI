Lyre never copies or changes your audio files. It remembers where they are.

1. Open **Track library**, press **Add tracks** and choose **Add files…**, **Add a folder…** (with its subfolders) or **Watch a folder…** (new files in it appear by themselves). You can also drop files and folders anywhere on the page.
2. Click a track's row, or press `Enter`, to open its **Details**: **Your title**, the title in the file, the file name and the folder.
3. To tag it, type in a tag group's box (**+ Add mood**, and the same for each group) and press `Enter` or `Tab`. Typing a new word adds it as a new tag.
4. To tag many tracks at once, select them (their boxes, or `Space` on a row), then press **Tag…**.

Use the search box to find titles, file names, artists and tags, and **Filters** to narrow the list by kind (Music, Ambience, FX), tag, energy, shape, folder or missing files.

Dynamic playlists and Live's **Find a track or playlist** both work from tags, so a few good tags save time at the table. A track is marked **New** until you tag it.

Where a track is offered depends on its **Type** in its Details (Music, Ambience or FX). FX tracks are offered for pads, and Ambience tracks for a scene's ambience. To change several, select them and use **⋯** › **Type**. In **Filters** › **Folders**, use a folder's **⋯** menu to choose what type its new tracks are, or to remove the folder.

To take a track out of Lyre and keep its file, choose **⋯** › **Remove from library** on its row. You can bring it back with **Undo**. Choose **Delete file from disk…** to send the file to the Recycle Bin.
