title: Play next, Add to queue and Repeat
group: Run a session
words: queue add to queue play next up next list order repeat repeat one loop room skip clear saved
---
Each room plays a list for the evening: the scene's music (or a playlist) in its order, and what you add to it.

- To play a track or playlist right after the track playing now, press **Play next** on it in Live.
- With **Add to queue**, the track or playlist goes to the bottom of the room's list, after everything already there.
- **Up next**, in the room's part of On air, shows the list from the next track to the bottom. Press **×** to take one out for this evening, or **Clear added** to take out everything you added.
- An ambience has **Play next** and **Add to queue** too. It waits at the bottom of Up next as **Ambience next**, and plays when you press **Next** on the room's ambience. An ambience loops until you change it.
- Starting another scene clears what you added. If you undo the scene change, what you added comes back.

Lyre has a **Repeat** for each room and a **Repeat one** for a scene's track.

- **Repeat** in On air, and on the Now playing bar, is for what plays in that room now: off, all, then one. It is never saved and ends when that room's music stops.
- **Repeat one** on a track of a scene is saved with the scene. The track plays again when it ends, each time the scene plays. When it is on, the button has a small dot.

With **Repeat all**, what you added comes round again with the rest. A scene's music always starts again from the top.
