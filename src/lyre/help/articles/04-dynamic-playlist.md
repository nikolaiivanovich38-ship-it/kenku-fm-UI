A dynamic playlist is made of the tracks that match its rules. It grows as you tag more tracks.

1. Open **Playlists** and press **New playlist**.
2. Choose **Dynamic**, then **Music**, **Ambience** or **FX**.
3. Under **Rules**, add the tags a track must have. Use **Shape** to narrow the playlist to tracks that build, fall away and so on. With no shape pressed, any shape matches.
4. **Matching tracks** lists the tracks in the playlist now.

To put the tracks in an order of your own, press **Make a manual copy**. Lyre makes a Manual playlist of the tracks that match now.

A **Manual** playlist has the tracks you pick, in the order you drag them into. Add tracks from its page, or drop audio files on it.
