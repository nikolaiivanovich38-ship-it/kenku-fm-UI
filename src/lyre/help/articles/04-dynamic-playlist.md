A dynamic playlist holds every track that matches its rules, and grows as you tag more tracks.

1. Open **Playlists** and press **New playlist**.
2. Choose **Dynamic**, then **Music**, **Ambience** or **FX**.
3. Under **Rules**, add the tags a track must have. **Shape** narrows it to tracks that build, fall away and so on; none pressed means any shape.
4. **Matching tracks** shows what it holds now.

To put the tracks in an order of your own, press **Make a manual copy**: it makes a Manual playlist of the tracks that match today.

A **Manual** playlist holds the tracks you pick, in the order you drag them to. Add tracks from its page, or drop audio files on it.
