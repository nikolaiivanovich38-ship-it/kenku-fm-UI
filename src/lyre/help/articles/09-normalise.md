Lyre can even out how loud your tracks are as it plays them. It never changes your files: it measures each track once and keeps the measurement in its library.

1. Library › **⋯** beside **Add tracks** › **Normalise volume…**.
2. Turn on **Normalise volume for the whole library** and choose a **Target level**: Quiet, Standard or Loud.

A playlist (in its Details) and a track (Details › How it plays) have **Normalise volume** too: **Library setting**, **On** or **Off**. The track's own choice wins, then its playlist's, then the library's.

A track not measured yet plays as it is. Hover a track's name in the Now playing bar to see how much it is turned up or down.
