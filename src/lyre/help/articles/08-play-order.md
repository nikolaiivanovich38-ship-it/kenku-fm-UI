Use the play order button, two small icons beside a playlist, to choose how it plays: **Shuffle**, then **No repeat**, **Repeat all** or **Repeat one**.

The button is on each playlist in Playlists and on the playlist's page, in a scene's music and the session and campaign lists, in Live's scene music, and in the Now playing bar.

- On the Playlists page, the choice is the playlist's own order.
- In a scene, a session's list or a campaign's list, the choice is for that scene or list only. There the button has an accent edge and a dot. To remove the choice, press **Use the playlist's own order here**.

When no choice is made, a playlist on its own plays in order and repeats all. In a scene it plays in order, and then the scene moves on.

- **Repeat one**: the same track plays until you press Next.
- **Repeat all**: the playlist starts again after its last track.
- **No repeat**: in a scene, the scene moves on. Played on its own, the playlist stops.
