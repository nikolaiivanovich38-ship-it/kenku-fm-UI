The play order button, two small icons by a playlist, chooses how it plays: **Shuffle**, then **No repeat**, **Repeat all** or **Repeat one**.

It is on each playlist in Playlists and on its page, in Live's scene music, and in the Now playing bar.

- On the Playlists page, it is the playlist's own order.
- In a scene, a session's list or a campaign's list, the choice is for that place only. The button shows an accent edge and dot there. **Use the playlist's own order here** takes the choice away.

With no choice made, a playlist on its own plays in order and repeats all; in a scene it plays in order, then the scene moves on.

- **Repeat one** plays the same track until you press Next.
- **Repeat all** starts the playlist again after its last track.
- **No repeat**: in a scene, the scene moves on; on its own, the playlist stops.
