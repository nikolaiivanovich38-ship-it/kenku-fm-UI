The **Now playing** bar has **Back** and **Queue** for what the players hear and for your preview. Previewing a playlist plays it through in your headphones, in its play order.

- Press **Back** to go to the previous track. After the first 3 seconds of a track, pressing **Back** starts that track again instead.
- To go to the next track, press **Next** on a room's row, or **Forward** on your preview's row.
- Press **Queue** to open the list of what played, what plays now and what comes next. Press the play button on a row to play that track now, and the list continues from there. To close the list, press `Esc`.

In Live, you can also play a track now from a room's **Up next**, with the play button on its row.
