Live is where you run the evening. **Run this session** on a campaign or session page loads it here.

- **On air**, at the top: a part for each room, with what it plays, **Up next**, its volume and its bot. Click a track's position line to jump in it. Its bot's button changes the channel; **+** adds a room.
- **Scenes**: press `Space`, or **Next scene**, to start the next scene. Click a scene's name to check it; anything in it plays alone without starting the scene. **Undo**, or `Ctrl+Z`, takes back the last thing you did within 8 seconds.
- **Current scene**: the running scene's music in play order, its ambience and its pads, which answer to their keys.
- **This session** and **Whole campaign**: their playlists and pads, to play a whole playlist, one of its tracks or a pad at any time.
- **Find a track or playlist** (`/`): search the whole library for anything not in the plan.
- **Fade all** (`Shift+Esc`) fades everything out over 5 seconds; **Stop all** stops at once.

A room is a voice channel with one of your bots in it. Scenes always start in the first room. With two rooms or more, **To:** on a box chooses where it plays. **No room** or **Nobody there** means the players hear nothing: the button beside it puts the bot where the players are.

**‹** (Back), top left on every screen, goes back one step through everywhere you have been, each as you left it. `Alt+Left` does the same. `Alt+1` to `Alt+7` jump to the boxes, from On air down.

**Keys** lists every key. If single keys get in your way, turn **Single-key shortcuts** off there.
