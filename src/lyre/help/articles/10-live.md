Live is where you run the evening. **Run this session** on a campaign or session page loads it here.

- **Scenes**: press `Space`, or **Next scene**, to start the next scene. Changed your mind? **Undo**, or `Ctrl+Z`, within a few seconds.
- **Off script**: search the whole library (`/` jumps there) for anything not in the plan, then play it.
- **On air**: one box per room, with what it plays, Pause and its volume.
- **Pads**: the scene's pad boards. Press a pad, or its key.
- **To:** on a box chooses which room it plays in, when you have more than one.
- **Fade all** (`Shift+Esc`) fades everything out; **Stop all** stops at once.

**Keys** lists every key. If single keys get in your way, turn **Single-key shortcuts** off there; `Shift+Esc` and `Ctrl+Z` still work.

Scenes always start in the first room.
