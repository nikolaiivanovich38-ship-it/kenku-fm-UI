When something goes wrong, a diagnostics file helps us find out why.

1. Press **Help** (or `F1`), then **Save diagnostics…** at the foot.
2. Choose a folder. Lyre saves one zip file there and names it.
3. Attach the zip to your bug report.

The zip holds Lyre's version, your Windows version, the last lines of Lyre's log, its settings, and any warnings about blocked content.

Your bot tokens and other secrets are taken out, and so is your user name in folder paths. Your music and your library are not in it.

Nothing is sent anywhere: the file stays where you saved it until you send it yourself.
