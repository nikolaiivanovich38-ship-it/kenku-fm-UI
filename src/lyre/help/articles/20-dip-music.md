group: Run a session

When an effect plays, Lyre can make the music quieter for a moment, so that a thunder crack or a roar is heard. While the music is dipped, the room's music line in On air shows **Dipped**.

- In **Settings › Sound to Discord › Dip the music under effects**, use **How far** (**Off**, **A little** or **A lot**) and **Comes back** to choose how much the music dips and how fast it comes back. Tick **Ambience dips too** to dip the ambience as well.
- **One-shot** and **Restart on press** pads dip the music. **Toggle loop** and **Hold to play** pads do not. To change this for one pad, choose **Dip the music** or **Don't dip** under **While it plays** on its pad board.
- A Short cue played over the music dips it in the same way.
