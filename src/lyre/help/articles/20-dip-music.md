When an effect plays, Lyre can make the music quieter for a moment, so that a thunder crack or a roar is heard. While the music is dipped, the room's music line in On air shows **Dipped**.

- In **Settings › Sound to Discord › Dip the music under effects**, choose how far the music dips (**Off**, **A little** or **A lot**), how fast it comes back, and whether ambience dips too.
- Pads that play once dip the music. Loops and pads you hold down to play do not.
- A Short cue played over the music dips it in the same way.
