Lyre plays to your players through a Discord bot. You make the bot once, and Lyre logs it in.

1. Open **Settings** in the menu. Under Discord bots, press **Add a bot**. Three steps follow.
2. **Create it**: press **Open the Discord Developer Portal**. In Discord, choose **New Application**, name it and press **Create**. Back in Lyre, press **Next**.
3. **Invite it**: in Discord, open **General Information** and copy the Application ID. Paste it into Lyre and press **Open the invite**. Pick your server and press **Authorize**. The invite lists only the View Channels, Connect and Speak permissions.
4. **Connect it**: in Discord, open **Bot**, press **Reset Token**, then **Copy**. Paste the token into Lyre and press **Connect**, then **Done**. Lyre keeps the token on this computer, encrypted, and shows only its last four characters.
5. Open **Rooms**. Your bot is listed as **Not in a room**. Press **Choose a room…** and pick a voice channel, or press **Add** beside the channel.

To move the bot during a game, choose another room. The sound keeps playing. Press **Leave** to take the bot out of the room, and **Undo** to put it back with the music.

For a second room (a side scene, a split party), press **Add a bot** again and make a second bot.

If you used Kenku FM, Lyre brings your bot across on its first start and keeps its token encrypted. If Kenku FM kept the token unprotected, a note in Settings › Discord bots says so once and explains how to make a new one.
