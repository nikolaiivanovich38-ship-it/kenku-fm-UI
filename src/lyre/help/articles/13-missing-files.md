When you move or rename audio files, Lyre can no longer find them. Their rows in the Track library say **Missing**, and **Filters** › **Missing** lists them.

1. Open **Track library**, press **⋯** beside **Add tracks** and choose **Find missing files…**.
2. **Look in a folder**: type a folder, choose one, or drop it on the page.
3. Lyre matches files by name, by content, and by sound (the same length and loudness), and shows why for each file. A sure match is ticked. A match Lyre is not sure of (the same name with another size, or only the same sound under another name) is not ticked, so preview it first.
4. Press **Relink** (the button shows how many files). If a match was wrong, press **Undo** to put them back.
