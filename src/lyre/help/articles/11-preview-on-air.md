title: Preview or play on air
group: Run a session

Every sound in Lyre says where it goes.

- **Preview** plays in your headphones only. Its tag reads **Only you**.
- **Play on air** plays for the players. Its tag reads **On air**, with the room's name when you have two or more rooms. **On air · no room** means no bot is in a voice channel: nobody hears it.

The **Now playing** bar, at the foot of the screen, shows what plays: a row per room and a row for your preview, each with its own Stop and volume. A paused row turns grey. The bar goes when everything stops.

In Live, the On air boxes show what each room hears, so the bar there shows only your preview.
