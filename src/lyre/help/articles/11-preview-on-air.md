Sounds in Lyre have a tag that shows where they play.

- **Preview** plays in your headphones only. Its tag reads **Only you**.
- **Play on air** plays for the players. Its tag reads **On air**, with the room's name when you have two or more rooms. **On air · no room** means no bot is in a voice channel. **On air · nobody there** means nobody is in the room's channel. Either way, nobody hears it.

The **Now playing** bar, at the bottom of the screen, shows what is playing: a row for each room and a row for your preview, each with a Stop button and a volume. Click or drag a row's position line to jump within the track. With the keyboard, Left and Right move 5 seconds. A paused row turns grey. The bar disappears when everything stops.

In Live, the On air boxes show what each room hears, so the bar there shows only your preview.

With **Split one headset** on (Settings › Headphones and speakers), the preview plays in one ear and On air in the other, but only while something plays on air. A preview on its own plays in both ears.
