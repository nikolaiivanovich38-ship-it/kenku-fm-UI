Library › **⋯** beside **Add tracks** › **Back up, move or share…**.

**Save to a file** makes one .lyre file of the whole library, one campaign, session, scene, playlist or pad board, or the tags only. Tick **Include the audio files** to take the music with it, for another computer or another game master.

**Open a file**: drop a .lyre file on the page, or choose one. Lyre shows what is in it and what you have already. For each of those, choose **Skip**, **Merge** or **Keep both**, then press **Bring in**.

A file that is not a Lyre file, or is damaged, is refused, and nothing in your library changes.

Lyre also backs up its library by itself every day.
