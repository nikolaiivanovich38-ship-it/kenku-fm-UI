The Audio profile says where a track starts and ends, what happens at its end, and how it fades. Open it from a track's **Audio profile** link in the Library's Details, or from the wave button by a track in a scene. From a scene it opens on that scene's settings, and **Back to** the scene takes you there again.

- **Start** and **End**: drag them on the waveform, move them with the arrow keys, or type a time.
- **At the end**: loop back to the loop start, move to the next track, or stop.
- **Fades**: a fade in and a fade out, each with its length.
- **Markers**: notes on the waveform, such as where it peaks.
- `Space` plays from the playhead in your headphones only, as the profile says.

**Settings for** chooses whose settings you change: the track everywhere, or only in one playlist or scene. A place uses the track's settings until you press **Give this place its own settings**; **Back to the track's settings** undoes that.
