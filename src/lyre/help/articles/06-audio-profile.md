title: How a track plays (Audio profile)

In the Audio profile you choose where a track starts and ends, what happens at its end, and how it fades. Open it from a track's **Audio profile** link in the Track library's Details, or from the wave button by a track in a scene. Opened from a scene, it shows that scene's settings. To go back to the scene, press the **Back to** button, which has the scene's name on it.

- **Start** and **End**: drag them on the waveform, move them with the arrow keys (a tenth of a second; with `Shift`, a second), or type a time such as 5:12.4. Press **Zoom in** to widen the waveform and place them more exactly.
- **At the end**: loop back to the loop start, move to the next track, or stop.
- **Fades**: a fade in and a fade out, each with its length.
- **Markers**: notes on the waveform, such as where it peaks.
- **Start at**: where the track starts, wherever it plays (in a scene, in Live or with Play on air). Choose the Beginning or one of its markers.
- **Pick up where it stopped**: the track starts again where it was last stopped, wherever it plays. It is the same switch as in the Track library's How it plays.
- Press `Space` to play from the playhead in your headphones only, as the profile says. You hear each change at once, and the track keeps playing when you press **Back**.

Use **Settings for** to choose what you change: the track everywhere, or the track in one playlist or scene only. A playlist or scene uses the track's settings until you press **Use separate settings here**. Press **Back to the track's settings** to undo that.
