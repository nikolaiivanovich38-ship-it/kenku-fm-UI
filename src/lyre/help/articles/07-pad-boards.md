A pad board is a grid of sound effects, each on a pad you press or play with a key.

1. **Pad boards** › **New board**.
2. Click an empty slot, then **Add pad**. Give the pad a **Label** and its **Sound**: one file, several variants, or any effect with a tag.
3. Choose a **Play mode**: One-shot, Toggle loop, Hold to play or Restart on press.
4. To give it a key, click its key box and press a letter, a number or `F1` to `F12`.

**Preview** on a pad plays it in your headphones only; **Play on air** plays it for the players.

**Try pads** in the Pads header: while it is on, pressing a pad or its key plays it as it will in Live, in your headphones only. Hold a Hold to play pad. Press **Try pads** again to stop.

**Columns** and **Rows** change the grid. Pads that no longer fit go to **Spare pads**; nothing is deleted.

Add the board to a scene, a session or a campaign, and it shows in Live's **Pads**.
