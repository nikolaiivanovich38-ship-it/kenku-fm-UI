A pad board is a grid of sound effects, each on a pad you press or play with a key.

1. Open **Pad boards** and press **New board**.
2. Click an empty slot, then **Add pad**. Give the pad a **Label** and its **Sound**: one file, several variants, or any effect with a tag.
3. Choose a **Play mode**: One-shot, Toggle loop, Hold to play or Restart on press.
4. To give it a key, click its key box and press a letter, a number or `F1` to `F12`.

Press **Preview** on a pad to hear it in your headphones only, or **Play on air** to play it for the players.

Turn on **Try pads** in the Pads header to hear the board as it will play in Live, in your headphones only. Click a pad or press its key. Hold down a Hold to play pad. Press **Try pads** again to stop.

Change the size of the grid with **Columns** and **Rows**. Pads that no longer fit go to **Spare pads**, and nothing is deleted.

Add the board to a scene, a session or a campaign, and it shows in Live's **Pads**.
