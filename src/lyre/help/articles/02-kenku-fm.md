On its first start, Lyre copies your Kenku FM library. Kenku FM's own files stay as they were, so you can go back to it.

Library › **⋯** beside **Add tracks** › **What came across from Kenku FM** shows:

- what Lyre copied and where it is now: playlists and their tracks, soundboards (now pad boards, their sounds now pads);
- **Bookmarks**: your Kenku FM bookmarks, each with **Copy**. Websites can't be opened in Lyre for now; paste the address into your browser;
- **Files Lyre cannot find**, with **Find them…**;
- **Not brought across**: what Lyre left behind.

Your Discord bot comes across too. Lyre keeps its token encrypted; Settings › Discord bots tells you once if it moved it.

Lyre is one window. What Kenku FM's player did is in Lyre's menu: playlists in **Playlists**, soundboards in **Pad boards**, playing to your players in **Live**. Sending a microphone to Discord and Stream Deck buttons are not in Lyre for now.
