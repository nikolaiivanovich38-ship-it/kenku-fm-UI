On its first start, Lyre copies your Kenku FM library. Kenku FM's own files stay as they were, so you can go back to it.

Library › **⋯** beside **Add tracks** › **What came across from Kenku FM** shows:

- what Lyre copied and where it is now: playlists and their tracks, soundboards (now pad boards, their sounds now pads) and bookmarks (website tabs);
- **Files Lyre cannot find**, with **Find them…**;
- **Not brought across**: what Lyre left behind.

The old player is still there as the **Lyre Player** tab, with your Kenku FM playlists and soundboards.
