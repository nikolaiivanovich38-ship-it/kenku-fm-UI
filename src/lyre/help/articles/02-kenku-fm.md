On its first start, Lyre copies your Kenku FM library. Kenku FM's files are not changed, so you can still go back to Kenku FM.

To see what was copied, open **Track library**, press **⋯** beside **Add tracks** and choose **What came across from Kenku FM**. The screen lists:

- what Lyre copied and where it is now: playlists and their tracks, and soundboards, which are now pad boards with their sounds as pads;
- **Bookmarks**: your Kenku FM bookmarks, each with **Copy**. Lyre cannot open websites for now, so copy the address into your browser;
- **Files Lyre cannot find**, with **Find them…**;
- **Not brought across**: what Lyre left behind.

Your Discord bot comes across too, and Lyre keeps its token encrypted. If Lyre moved the token, a note in Settings › Discord bots says so once.

Lyre has one window. What Kenku FM's player window did is now in Lyre's menu. Playlists are in **Playlists**, soundboards are in **Pad boards**, and you play to your players in **Live**. Lyre cannot send a microphone to Discord or use Stream Deck buttons for now.
