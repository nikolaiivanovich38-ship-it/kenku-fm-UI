In **Filters**, click a filter once to show only those tracks (✓). Click it a second time to hide them (struck out), and a third time to clear it. "Tense · not Combat" at the top of the box shows which filters are on. Press **Clear all filters** to clear them all. Folders work the same way: click once to show only that folder, and twice to hide it.

To sort the filters, press **Order the filters** at the top of Filters and choose tag list order, A to Z, Z to A, or most tracks first.

Use **Columns** to choose what the list shows: artist, album, type, **Use it for**, tags, shape, energy, length, **Plays for** (the length after the Audio profile's start and end), added, folder. Click a column's name to sort by it, and click again to reverse the order.
